Foundational Thesis

Demographic Dividend at the Operating Layer

“Policy is only as good as its execution.”

Hrishikesh A. C. Wakhare works at the granular operating layer of India's demographic dividend as Public Policy & Development Practitioner with the Ex-Government of Odisha. In Dhenkanal district, he converges 13 distinct line departments and 14 implementing agencies into concrete outcomes that youth can tangibly experience.

In parallel, his fellowships at Citizens for Public Leadership (CPL) and IMPRI (Impact and Policy Research Institute) build the analytical counterpart: investigating how India’s grassroots skilling story translates into its 21st-century public diplomacy and soft-power capabilities.
