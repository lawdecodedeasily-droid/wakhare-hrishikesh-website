The Convergence Table

A high-velocity, worldwide working table for policy and governance practitioners. Bring a real structural problem, argue it without bureaucratic posture, and leave with something immediately usable.

• Not a webinar • Not a panel talk • Not a dying group chat
The Problem with Policy Forums

Why Most Think-Tanks and Panels Fail Practitioners

Most governance conferences consist of prepared slide-decks and ceremonial pleasantries. By the time a session ends, no one has challenged an assumption, and no operating protocol has been altered.

The Convergence Table was created to reverse this. It is a working session where participants present an unresolved operational deadlock—whether it’s district inter-departmental conflict, data leakage across implementing agencies, or narrative dissonance in public diplomacy—and subject it to peer critique.

The Ground Rules

Three Inviolable Precepts

1. Substance Over Seniority

Hierarchy is checked at the door. An IAS officer, a grassroots fellow, and an international researcher speak with identical standing.

2. Bring a Friction, Not a Trophy

Do not present sanitized case studies. Bring the specific mechanism that broke and the operational trade-offs you are wrestling with.

3. Usable Artifacts Only

Every session concludes with a single-page blueprint, an automated sheet template, or a refined clause ready for ground testing.
